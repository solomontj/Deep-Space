# DeepSpace
  by Solomon Jones, Adam Shaar, Olivia Guzik, and Kathleen Melonashi

"Deep Space" is a dynamic puzzle game set in the year 2245, providing an immersive escape
room experience in a futuristic space setting. Players awake aboard the IEE (Infinity's Edge
Explorer), a spacecraft on a critical exploratory mission far from Earth. The mission has
encountered severe disruptions, and the ship is in jeopardy with flashing lights and
malfunctioning systems. Players must navigate a series of coding-related challenges to repair
and regain control of the ship. These tasks include unlocking doors, fixing electrical faults,
cracking computer passwords, and reactivating the ship's AI robot. The ultimate goal is to
restore communication with Earth by aligning satellites and beam computers, ensuring the
player can redirect the ship home.
